# Quartiers d'Hiver

Quartiers d’Hiver is a first-year master's group project that reimagines an abandoned 1960s brutalist pavilion in Parc Angrignon, in Montreal. Originally built as a winter shelter for zoo animals, the landmark concrete structure is adapted into a sports and community center while reusing as much of the existing concrete as possible. Two new modern volumes slide into the shell, setting up a clear dialogue between past and present. The basement concentrates the tennis courts and uses the full height of the space, while the ground floor supports community life and public programs. All new construction is demountable glulam, and a simple landscape strategy reconnects the site to its forested surroundings.
